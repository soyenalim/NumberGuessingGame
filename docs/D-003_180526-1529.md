### ISSUE #1 | Replace binary feedback with temperatur mechanic

Initially I thought of a 8-tier feedback because I wanted a more specific feedbad. My reasoning was more tiers = more information = fun game

🔥 FIRE | 3 steps in range

🌶️ HOT | 5 steps in range

♨️ WARM | 6 steps in range

⛅ MILD | 7 steps in range

🍃 COOL | 8 steps in range

😶‍🌫️ CHILL | 9 steps in range

⛄ FREEZING | 10 steps in range

🧊 FROST | everything else

#### CONFLICT 1-001 ⚠️

The tiers grew linearly, which meant the middle tiers blur together. A player at distance 20 and a player with distance 28 recieved different feedback but the practicality was minimal, it's not helpful it's noise. And too much vocabulary would be hard to remember.

##### DECISION ✅ 

- ✅ 5 Tiers
- ✅ Non-linear & exponential progression. 
    - ✅ Tight range = close
    - ✅ Wide range = far

| **Tier**   | **Range** | **Size**   |
|------------|-----------|------------|
| 🔥 Fire     |    1-3    | 3 Numbers  |
| ♨️ Warm     |    4-5    | 2 Numbers  |
| ⛅ Mild     |    6-15   | 10 Numbers |
| ☃️ Cold     |   16-25   | 10 Numbers |
| 🧊 Freezing |    26+    | 25 Numbers |
| -  | -          |   **50**   |

- Proximity = Precision : When you're close you should be rewarded carefully. A 1 number difference stays the same but a 3 number leap changes tier and creates tension
- Distance = Vagueness :  The player doesn't need 6 different ways for 'bad'.
- Vocabular clearness

> In a range of 0 - 100, 50 is the farthest you can ever be from a target.
>> Fire + Warm is 5 numbers = 10% of max distance
>> Mild + Cold is 20 numbers = 40% of max distance
>> Freezing is 25 numbers = 50% of max distance
>> It's closer feel of probability.

> Tier system will live in it's own method, isolated from input validation.
>>- If game too hard, ranges can widen easily. Doesn't touch `validate()`
>>- If game adds a new mode like *HARD MODE*, thresholds can scale or get replaced easily with a percentage based one.

## CONSEQUENCES 🚨

- *POSITIVE*
    - Simpler vocabulary reduces cognitive load; easier to test edge cases

- *NEGATIVE*
    - Less granularity means some players might feel "stuck in Mild forever"

- *RISKS*
    - 10-number Mild range might feel too wide; may need tuning after playtesting
